# Vision

A droning, meditative visual that breathes with its own sound. A low saw-wave oscillator generates a continuous tone; its frequencies drive evolving noise patterns through a feedback loop with slow rotation and zoom. Bloom catches the bright edges and pulses with the audio energy.

## Aesthetic Goals

- Dark ambient feel — low brightness punctuated by glowing highlights
- Slow organic movement from feedback rotation and zoom
- Bass frequencies create large-scale pattern shifts
- High frequencies trigger bloom glow at the edges
- Meditative, not frenetic — smoothing keeps everything fluid

## Techniques Demonstrated

- Synth-generated audio (headless-compatible, no microphone needed)
- Frequency-band analysis driving visual parameters
- Feedback loop with zoom and rotation for evolving patterns
- Bloom as audio-reactive glow
