# Vision

Render a spinning 3D cube floating in space with proper lighting and an orbiting camera view.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Clean, geometric aesthetic with flat shading
- Red-orange cube against a dark background
- Smooth orbital camera motion for cinematic feel

## Techniques Demonstrated
- SceneComposer for 3D geometry management
- CameraOperator with orbital positioning
- DirectionalLight for scene illumination
- Render3D pipeline setup and configuration
