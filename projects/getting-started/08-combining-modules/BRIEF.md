# Vision

Build an audio-reactive 3D scene where geometric shapes pulse, orbit, and glow in response to music frequencies.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Dynamic, music-driven color shifts on 3D objects
- Bass-responsive scaling for rhythmic punch
- Glowing bloom that intensifies with high frequencies

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Integrating Audio, Render3D, and Core modules together
- Mapping frequency bands to 3D transforms and colors
- Applying 2D post-processing to 3D renders
- Real-time scene manipulation in update()
