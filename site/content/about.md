## Why Vivid exists

Live visuals have great tools, but most of them treat sound and picture as separate worlds bolted
together after the fact. Vivid starts from the opposite premise: **sound and picture are one signal
path you perform.** It is a new instrument for live visuals — play them together, rewire the path
between them live, author your own operators with code and AI, and fork the whole thing.

The built-in looks are teaching examples. The real idea is that you make your own — and that an AI
agent can build alongside you over MCP, not just tweak knobs. That is the north star the whole app is
plumbed for.
