Vivid is built in the open. The project lives on GitHub, and the best way to shape it right now is to
show up there — file an issue, open a discussion, or send a project.

## Where things happen

- **[GitHub repository](https://github.com/seethroughlab/vivid)** — source, releases, and the issue
  tracker. Bugs, feature ideas, and questions are all welcome here.
- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/seethroughlab/vivid/discussions)** — the place for
  open-ended questions, sharing what you're making, and talking through operator design.
- **Discord** &nbsp;<span class="badge-soon">coming soon</span> — a real-time space for performers and
  builders. We'll link it here once it opens.

## Share what you make

The [Gallery](/gallery/) is "Made with Vivid," attributed to the people who made it. Want your work in
it? Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/seethroughlab/vivid/discussions) with a clip and a
short note on how you built it, or email **jeff@seethroughlab.com**. Real attributions only — we would
rather ship a "coming soon" than a stock-photo wall.

## Contribute

Custom operators, tutorials, and fixes are all fair game. The [Learn](/learn/) path shows how projects
and operators are structured; the repository's issues and discussions are where to propose a change
before you build it.
