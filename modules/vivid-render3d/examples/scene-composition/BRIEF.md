# Vision

A temple-like arrangement with a central pillar topped by a golden orb, surrounded by a ring of colorful boxes that orbit and bob - demonstrating multi-object scene composition.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Architectural composition with clear focal point (glowing golden sphere)
- Harmonious color gradient across the surrounding boxes
- Gentle floating motion creates a serene, meditative atmosphere

## Techniques Demonstrated
- SceneComposer for managing multiple primitives with individual transforms
- Procedural placement using trigonometric functions for circular arrangement
- Per-entry transform animation for individual object motion
- Orbital camera with height oscillation for dynamic viewpoint
