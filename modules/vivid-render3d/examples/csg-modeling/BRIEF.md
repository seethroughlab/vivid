# Vision

An exhibition of CSG (Constructive Solid Geometry) operations showing how complex shapes emerge from simple primitives - a hollow sphere, a rounded cube, and a charming snowman figure.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Sculptural, museum-like presentation of three distinct CSG techniques
- Warm, soft lighting with subtle bloom for a refined look
- Playful wobbling animation on the snowman to add personality

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Boolean Subtract: outer sphere minus inner sphere creates hollow shell
- Boolean Intersect: box intersected with sphere creates rounded corners
- Visual union: overlapping spheres compose the snowman body and head
- Mouse-controlled camera orbit with dynamic distance adjustment
