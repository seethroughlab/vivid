# Vision

A colorful showcase of fundamental 3D primitives orbiting in space, featuring a hollow cube created through CSG subtraction as the centerpiece.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Vibrant, candy-colored geometric forms (pink torus, green cylinder, orange cone, blue hollow cube)
- Gentle, hypnotic rotations that reveal each shape's unique geometry
- Clean, dark background that lets the colors pop

## Techniques Demonstrated
- SceneComposer for organizing multiple primitives with transforms and colors
- CSG Boolean subtraction (box minus sphere creates hollow cube)
- Orbital camera animation for continuous scene exploration
- Per-object transform animation via scene entry manipulation
