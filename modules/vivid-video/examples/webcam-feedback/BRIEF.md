# Vision

A hypnotic feedback loop with time-delayed echoes, creating spiraling trails and ghostly duplicates that follow your movements with an adjustable delay.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Psychedelic spiral motion from continuous rotation
- Ghostly time-echo creating a sense of temporal layering
- Ethereal, dreamlike quality with overlapping past and present

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Feedback operator with zoom and rotation for spiral trails
- FrameCache storing 1.5 seconds of history
- TimeMachine for uniform time-delay echo effect
- Composite blending (Screen mode) for luminous overlap
