# Vision

A live VJ performance toolkit with multi-layer video mixing, explosive geometry flashes, bold typography hits, and real-time blend mode switching for dynamic visual sets.

## Aesthetic Goals
- High-energy, beat-drop visuals with strobing flashes and color pops
- Bold neon color palette: hot pink, cyan, gold, purple, neon green
- Layered depth with crossfading between video pairs and feedback trails
- Typography that punches through with words like "DROP", "BASS", "VIVID"

## Techniques Demonstrated
- 4-layer Composite mixing with multiple blend modes (Add, Screen, Multiply, Difference)
- Canvas API for procedural geometry (circles, triangles, rings) and text rendering
- Feedback operator for motion trails
- Chromatic aberration triggered by flash events
