# Vision

Repeating patterns and kaleidoscopic symmetry from a nature image, with scrolling tiles and rotating mirror segments.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Seamless wallpaper-like repetition
- Mirrored edges for seamless tiling
- Multi-segment kaleidoscope symmetry
- Slow color hue drift

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Tile operator with and without mirroring
- Transform for rotation before tiling
- Mirror operator in Quad and Kaleidoscope modes
- Animated segment count variation
- Mouse control for tile density and scroll speed
