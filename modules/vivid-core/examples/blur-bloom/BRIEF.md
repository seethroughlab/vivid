# Vision

Glowing geometric shapes floating against a dark void, demonstrating cinematic post-processing effects that add warmth and atmosphere.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Warm glowing orbs (orange, blue, green) with soft halos
- Dark cinematic background with edge vignetting
- Gentle floating motion creating a dreamy, meditative quality

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Blur operator for soft focus effects
- Bloom for luminous glow halos
- Vignette for cinematic framing
- Multi-shape compositing with additive blending
