# Vision

Experimental visual effects showcase featuring pixelated mosaics, particle networks, film grain textures, and strobe flashes.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Retro pixelated aesthetics with animated block sizes
- Organic particle constellation networks
- Vintage film texture with colored grain
- Rhythmic flash punctuation

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Pixelate for mosaic/retro effects
- Plexus for particle network visualization
- FilmGrain for vintage film look
- Flash for triggered strobe events
- Fullscreen presentation mode
