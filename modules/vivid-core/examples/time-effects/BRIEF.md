# Vision

Slit-scan and temporal displacement effects that stretch video across time, creating surreal warped imagery from frame history.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Classic horizontal slit-scan distortion
- Radial time warp (center-to-edge delay)
- Organic noise-based time displacement
- Visible temporal smearing of motion

## Techniques Demonstrated
- FrameCache for storing frame history
- TimeMachine with gradient displacement maps
- Vertical, horizontal, and radial slit-scan
- Noise-based organic time distortion
- Animated depth parameter
