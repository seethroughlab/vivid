# Vision

A cosmic nebula effect with swirling rainbow particles around a bright white core, enhanced with bloom for ethereal glow.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Dense swirling particle clouds with rainbow colors
- Bright pulsing core with white-to-blue gradient
- Ethereal bloom glow for depth
- Mouse-interactive attractor strength

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Particles operator with attractor physics
- Two-layer particle composition (swirl + core)
- Additive composite blending
- Bloom post-processing
- Mouse control for real-time parameter adjustment
