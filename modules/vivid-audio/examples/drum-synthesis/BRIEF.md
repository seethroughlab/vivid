# Vision

A 3D audio-visual experience where synthesized drums drive a PBR-rendered asteroid and ring system with bold 2D graphic overlays.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Rotating asteroid with craggy noise displacement
- Saturn-like ring that pulses with snare hits
- Bold rectangular bars that appear behind and inside the asteroid silhouette
- Dramatic rim lighting and bloom effects

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Clock and Sequencer triggering Kick, Snare, HiHat, Clap
- 3D PBR rendering with SceneComposer
- DepthMask for 2D/3D compositing effects
- Audio-synchronized camera movement and lighting
