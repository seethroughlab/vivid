# Vision

A fully-featured drum machine with pattern sequencing, Euclidean rhythms, and visual hit feedback through pulsing colored shapes.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Four colored circles representing kick (red), snare (gold), hi-hat (cyan), and clap (magenta)
- Shapes pulse and brighten on each drum hit
- Minimal dark background emphasizing the rhythm visualization

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Clock and Sequencer for pattern-based triggering
- Euclidean sequencer as alternative rhythm generator
- 808-style synthesized drums (Kick, Snare, HiHat, Clap)
- ImGui control panel for real-time parameter adjustment
