# Vision

A structured musical composition with sections (intro, verse, chorus, outro) that drive visual changes synced to song form.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Visual intensity that builds through song sections
- Calm intro/outro with larger, slower noise
- Building verse with increasing scale
- Intense chorus with rapid, dense patterns
- Flash on new bar for structural emphasis

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Song operator for section-based arrangement
- Clock synchronization with bar/beat tracking
- Sequencer for drum patterns
- Section-aware visual parameter modulation
