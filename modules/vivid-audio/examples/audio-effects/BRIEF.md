# Vision

An interactive audio effects showcase where mouse position selects and controls five classic audio processors applied to a synth melody.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Clean, informative spectrum visualization
- Color-coded effects with smooth transitions
- Dark interface with vibrant frequency bars

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Compressor, Phaser, Flanger, Bitcrush, Overdrive effects
- FFT spectrum analysis with logarithmic display
- Mouse-controlled effect selection and parameter modulation
