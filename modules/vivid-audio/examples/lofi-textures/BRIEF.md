# Vision

Vintage lo-fi audio textures with vinyl crackle, tape wobble, and bit-crushed warmth playing soft chord progressions.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Nostalgic, worn aesthetic
- Visual grain texture matching the audio character
- Slow-moving chord changes for contemplative mood
- Warm, degraded sonic palette

## Techniques Demonstrated
- TapeEffect with saturation, wow, flutter, and hiss
- Bitcrush for digital bit-reduction character
- Crackle for vinyl-style random impulses
- FilmGrain visual effect matching audio lo-fi aesthetic
