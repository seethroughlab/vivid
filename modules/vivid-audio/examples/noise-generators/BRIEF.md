# Vision

An exploration of noise colors and textures showing white, pink, and brown noise alongside filtered percussion and vinyl crackle.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Three colored bars (white, pink, brown) showing active noise level
- Rhythmic hi-hat triggered by clock
- Random crackle dot pulsing with vinyl texture
- Minimal dark interface emphasizing audio control

## Techniques Demonstrated
- NoiseGen with White, Pink, and Brown noise colors
- Filtered noise through highpass for hi-hat texture
- Decay envelope for percussive shaping
- Crackle for vinyl-style random impulses
