# Vision

A soft, lush pad synthesizer with vocal formant filtering that plays a D minor scale from the keyboard with randomly changing vowel sounds.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Warm, evolving color palette based on current vowel
- Central pulsing shape that responds to audio level
- Dreamy noise background with hue-shifting colors
- Immersive reverb-drenched atmosphere

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Detuned oscillator stack for thick pad sound
- Formant filter for vocal vowel character (A, E, I, O, U)
- Keyboard-as-piano input with musical frequency constants
- Envelope-controlled dynamics with long release
