# Vision

Evolving textural soundscapes created by granular synthesis, slowly scanning through an audio file with modulated grain parameters.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Ambient, meditative atmosphere
- Continuous sonic texture with gentle evolution
- Spatial depth through reverb and stereo spread

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Granular synthesis with position and pitch spray
- Hann windowing for smooth grain transitions
- Slow modulation of position, grain size, and pitch
- Reverb for atmospheric depth
