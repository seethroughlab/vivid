# Vision

A lush polyphonic wavetable synthesizer playing arpeggiated chords with morphing timbres, unison voices, and spatial effects.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Evolving sonic texture through wavetable position modulation
- Thick, wide stereo image from unison and chorus
- Spacious reverb tail for ambient feel
- Visual noise that responds to wavetable position

## Techniques Demonstrated
- WavetableSynth with polyphony and unison
- Filter envelope modulation for dynamic timbre
- LFO modulating wavetable position
- Chorus and Reverb for spatial enhancement
