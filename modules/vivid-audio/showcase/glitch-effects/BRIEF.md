# Vision

Eight tempo-synced glitch effects applied to a drum loop, from beat repeats and stutters to tape stops and frequency shifting.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Clean interface with effect indicator circles
- Audio level meter showing RMS and peak
- Color-coded effect selection that pulses with sound
- Dark background emphasizing the UI elements

## Techniques Demonstrated
- BeatRepeat, Reverse, Stutter, Scratch, TapeStop, FrequencyShift, Stretch, Glitch
- Tempo-synced effect triggering with clock divisions
- Mouse-controlled effect selection and parameter adjustment
- Canvas-based parameter visualization
