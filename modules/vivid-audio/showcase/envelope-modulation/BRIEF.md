# Vision

An educational demonstration of three envelope types showing how ADSR, AR, and pitch envelopes shape sound over time.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Animated bar that grows and shrinks with envelope value
- Stage indicator dot showing current envelope phase
- Color-coded demos: green (ADSR), orange (AR), purple (pitch sweep)
- Subtle gradient background that pulses with sound

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Envelope (ADSR) for sustained synth sounds
- AR (Attack-Release) for plucky percussion
- PitchEnv for kick drum pitch sweeps
- Filter cutoff modulation via envelope
