# Vision

A visual step sequencer showcasing all nine drum operators with a colorful 16-step grid, random rolls, skips, and master glitch effects.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Grid-based visualization with color-coded rows for each drum
- Playhead sweeping across the pattern with step flash effects
- Roll indicators and glitch intensity bar for visual feedback
- Each drum has a distinct color identity

## Techniques Demonstrated
- All drum operators: Kick, Snare, HiHat, Clap, Tom, Cymbal, FMDrum, Clang, DrumStack
- Pattern presets and randomization (skips, rolls)
- Glitch effect for creative audio mangling
- Canvas-based sequencer grid rendering
