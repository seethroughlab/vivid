# Vision

Audio-reactive visuals that pulse and shift colors in response to music from files or live microphone input.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Warm, organic color palette that breathes with bass frequencies
- Central shape that pulses on beat detection
- Layered noise textures modulated by mid and high frequencies
- Blooming glow effect that intensifies with energy

## Techniques Demonstrated
- FFT, Levels, BandSplit, and BeatDetect audio analysis
- Real-time input switching between file and microphone
- HSV color cycling synced to audio energy
