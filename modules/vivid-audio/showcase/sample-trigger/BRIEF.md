# Vision

A colorful 8-pad sample trigger interface where keyboard rows play samples at normal, pitched-up, or pitched-down variations.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Grid of 8 colored rectangular pads in rainbow hues
- Pads pulse and brighten when triggered
- Visual feedback follows drum pad layout conventions
- Dark background making colors pop

## Techniques Demonstrated
- SampleBank for loading sample folders
- SamplePlayer with polyphonic voice management
- Pitch variation for creative sample manipulation
- Reverb for spatial enhancement
