# Vision

Polyrhythmic drum patterns generated by the Euclidean algorithm, visualized as concentric pulsing rings.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Four nested rings in distinct colors (red, yellow, cyan, green)
- Rings pulse outward and thicken on each drum hit
- Slow rotation at different speeds creates visual counterpoint
- Bloom glow effect enhances the rhythmic pulses

## Techniques Demonstrated
- Euclidean pattern generator with classic rhythms (tresillo, bossa nova, samba)
- Mouse control for BPM and pattern complexity
- Audio-thread triggering via setTriggerSource pattern
- Drum envelope feedback for ring animation
