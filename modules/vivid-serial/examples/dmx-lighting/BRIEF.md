# Vision

Audio-reactive stage lighting with chase patterns, strobe triggers, and fog machine control for live music environments.

## Aesthetic Goals
- Bass-driven color pulses and brightness surges
- Beat-synchronized chase patterns across fixtures
- High-frequency triggered strobe effects
- Immersive concert/club atmosphere

## Techniques Demonstrated
- DMXOut controlling multiple fixture types
- FFT frequency bands mapped to lighting channels
- Clock-based chase sequencing
- Visual preview of DMX output
